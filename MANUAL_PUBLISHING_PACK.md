## 9. Claims to keep precise

Apple’s own safety guidance treats photo-location metadata as something users may choose to manage before sharing. [3] Private Preflight should therefore say it **inspects available metadata locally** and can make an explained local clean copy; it should not claim to identify every privacy risk in an image.

Similarly, use **“potential PII signals”**, **“formula-like cells”**, and **“visible local evidence”** for the CSV tool. Do not claim that it guarantees a CSV is safe, clean, or compliant.
